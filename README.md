# MediSuite – AI-Powered Hospital and Insurance Claim Management System

MediSuite is a web-based healthcare insurance claim management system designed to simplify the coordination between patients, hospitals and insurance providers.

The project focuses on automating document processing, supporting medical-code suggestions, validating insurance claims, detecting possible anomalies and managing claim review and settlement workflows.

## Key Modules

- User Authentication and Role Management
- Patient and Insurance Policy Management
- Pre-Authorization Management
- AI-Assisted Document Processing and OCR
- Claim Submission and Tracking
- Policy Validation and Anomaly Detection
- Insurance Officer Review and Approval
- Payment and Settlement Management
- Notifications, Reports and Audit Logs

## Project Team

Developed as an academic Software Engineering project by me.

## Project Scope

MediSuite is designed as a decision-support platform. AI assists with document extraction, medical-code suggestions and risk indicators, while final insurance claim decisions remain with authorized insurance officers.
